How it works

Step 1 / 4

A message arrives

Someone makes you a promise, or you take on a task. Usually it gets lost among hundreds of messages.

Step 2 / 4

AI reads it

Share a screenshot of the chat with Synvia AI. In seconds it finds who will do what, and by when.

Step 3 / 4

It lands in your list

The messy message becomes a tidy card with the person, date and time, right in your list. You type nothing.

Step 4 / 4

It reminds you on time

On Friday morning you get a notification. Mark it done or snooze it without opening the app. AI can even write Ahmet a polite reminder.
