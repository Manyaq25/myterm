Frequently asked

  • Where is my data stored?

    All your people and task/promise records are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to a cloud server.

  • How do I cancel my subscription?

    You can cancel anytime from the "Subscriptions" section of your App Store / Google Play account settings.

  • How do I delete my data?

    In the app, go to Settings → Data → "Delete My Account and All Data" to permanently erase all data on your phone. Since there's no sign-up, this button erases all your records. See our data deletion page for details.

  • How do the AI features work?

    When you give it a voice note, a screenshot, a PDF or some text, AI finds the tasks in it (who, what, when) and shows them to you so you can add them to your list. What you send is used only at that moment and is not stored.

  • What is an AI use?

    The free version gives you 6 AI uses every month. Finding the tasks in a piece of text, a voice note, a screenshot or a PDF, asking the assistant a question, or having it write a reminder message each uses 1. Premium is unlimited, and you can earn 3 extra uses for every friend you invite.
