Deleting your account and data

Unwrap · Developer: İbrahim Aydın · Last updated: October 24, 2026

In Unwrap, your chats, reports and Mila conversations are stored only on your phone, not on our server. The server holds only a small profile (name, avatar, details you typed in) and a session record, and only if you signed in with Apple. You can delete all of it from inside the app.

1. Deleting from inside the app

Labels below are as shown in the English version of the app; the Turkish labels are on the Turkish page.

Chats, reports and Mila conversations

  1. Open Unwrap and tap the Settings tab at the bottom.
  2. In the Your data section:
    • Delete all analyses: permanently deletes all your uploaded chats, reports and Mila conversations from this phone.
    • Delete Mila chats: deletes only your conversations with Mila; chats and reports stay.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Account (if you signed in with Apple, iPhone)

  1. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. Your profile and all sessions on the server are deleted immediately, you are signed out, and the profile details on your phone (name, avatar, typed-in details) are cleared.

Deleting your account does not delete the chats on your phone; use Delete all analyses above for those as well. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: cancel it in Settings → Manage subscription or in your store settings.

There is no account on Android, so no profile of yours is kept on the server. Your data is on your phone and is deleted with the options in Settings.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling Unwrap also deletes all chats, reports, Mila conversations and the profile photo in the app's folder. (On iPhone, small technical items in the Keychain, such as the sign-in token, may remain after uninstalling; they contain no chats.)

2. What is deleted, what is kept?

DataWhat happens
Chats, reports, Mila conversationsOn your phone; deleted immediately with “Delete all analyses” or by uninstalling. Never stored on our server.
Profile on the server (name, avatar, typed-in details)Deleted immediately with “Delete account”.
Session recordsDeleted immediately with “Delete account”.
Usage countersContain no chats; deleted automatically within 40 days at the latest.
App verification records (random device key / installation ID)Contain no chats or names; kept to prevent abuse; deleted on request by email.
AI replies you reportedStored masked and without a user ID; since they can't be linked to you, they can't be deleted individually. The newest 2,000 reports are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
Purchase recordsKept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies and legal obligations. On request, we will ask RevenueCat to delete your subscriber record; Apple's and Google's own records are under their control.

3. If the app is gone: request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email aydinibrahim25@gmail.com with the subject “Unwrap data deletion request” and include:

  • the platform you used (iPhone / Android) and whether you signed in with Apple;
  • if you subscribed, the store order number (the “Order ID” in Apple's receipt email, or the number starting with “GPA.” in Google Play). Our IDs are pseudonymous, so this is the only way we can find your record.

If we find your record, we delete your server profile, sessions, app verification records and RevenueCat subscriber record, and let you know the result within 30 days at the latest. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity. Since your chats were never on our server, there is nothing to delete about them there.

You can also stop using Sign in with Apple on iPhone via Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple → Unwrap → Stop Using Apple ID.
