Support & FAQ

What is Unwrap?

Unwrap turns the WhatsApp and Telegram chats you export into fun statistics and share cards, right on your phone: who texts more, who replies faster, which emojis come up most, and more. An Unwrap Pro subscription also unlocks AI reports and chat-specific conversations and roleplay with Mila, the relationship coach.

Unwrap is for users aged 18 and over. The app is currently being prepared for the App Store and Google Play.

Bringing a chat into Unwrap

  • How do I export a WhatsApp chat on iPhone?
    1. In WhatsApp, open the chat you want to analyze and tap the contact or group name at the top.
    2. Scroll to the bottom of the info page.
    3. Tap Export Chat (right above the options in red).
    4. Choose Without Media. We don't need the photos; the file stays small and the analysis is faster.
    5. Pick Unwrap in the share menu; the chat opens straight in Unwrap. If it's not in the list, swipe left and tap More, or choose Save to Files and then tap Choose file in Unwrap.
  • How do I export a WhatsApp chat on Android?
    1. In WhatsApp, open the chat and tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
    2. Tap More at the bottom of the menu.
    3. Choose Export chat.
    4. Choose Without media.
    5. In the share menu, save it to Files or Drive; then go back to Unwrap and pick the .txt file with Choose file.
  • How do I export a Telegram chat?

    Telegram's phone app can't export chats; this is only available in Telegram Desktop on a computer.

    1. Open Telegram Desktop on your computer and open the chat.
    2. From the ⋮ menu at the top right, click Export chat history.
    3. Uncheck photos and videos.
    4. Choose Machine-readable JSON as the format and click Export; a result.json file is created.
    5. Email the file to yourself or send it via Drive, then pick result.json in Unwrap with Choose file.
  • I get a "Couldn't read the file" message.

    Try exporting the chat again with the steps above: choose "Without Media" in WhatsApp, and JSON (not HTML) as the format in Telegram. If it still doesn't work, write to us.

Privacy

  • Where are my chats stored?

    The chats you import are opened, parsed and stored only on your phone; they are not uploaded to our server. You don't need an account to use Unwrap. Statistics and share cards are created entirely on your phone, without AI.

  • What is sent for an AI report?

    Only when you ask for a report or write to Mila, a selection of messages (up to 400) and summary statistics, not the whole chat, are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI for analysis. Phone numbers, emails, IBANs, card numbers, ID numbers and links are hidden first. This content is not stored on our server. Details: Privacy Policy.

Subscription

  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

    In Unwrap, tap Settings → Manage subscription, or go straight to your store settings: on iPhone, Settings → Apple ID (your name) → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

  • How does the free trial work?

    Eligible new subscribers may get a free trial; its length is shown on the purchase screen. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it converts to a paid subscription on the plan you chose.

  • Can I get a refund?

    Payments are taken by Apple and Google, and they handle refunds under their own policies; we can't issue refunds directly. On iPhone, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, use Google's Google Play refund page.

  • I got a new phone. How do I bring my subscription back?

    Open Unwrap on the new phone with the same Apple ID or Google account and tap Settings → Restore purchases. A subscription can be used on up to 4 devices. If you signed in with Apple on iPhone, your profile comes along too; chats stay on the old phone, so you'll need to import them again.

Your data and reporting content

  • How do I delete my data?

    Settings → Your data → Delete all analyses (all chats, reports and Mila conversations) or Delete Mila chats. If you signed in with Apple, Profile → Account → Delete account also deletes your small server profile right away. Details and requesting deletion by email: Account and data deletion.

  • How do I report an inappropriate AI reply?

    In a report, tap ⚑ Report content at the bottom of the report. In Mila, press and hold the reply you want to report. In the sheet that opens, pick a reason (Offensive, Sexual content, Harmful, Inaccurate, Other), add a short note if you like, and tap Send. Only that AI text is sent to us, never your chat. We review reports and use them to improve Mila.

  • How accurate are reports and Mila?

    The AI looks at only part of a chat; results are for entertainment and general guidance and may be wrong. They are not professional, psychological, medical or legal advice. If you feel that you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number right away (112 in Türkiye and the EU).
