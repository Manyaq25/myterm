Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

In short

  • The WhatsApp and Telegram chats you import are opened, parsed and stored on your phone only. You don't need an account to use Unwrap.
  • When you ask for an AI report or write to Mila, a selection of messages (up to 400 messages, each cut to 300 characters) and summary statistics, not the whole chat, are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI for analysis, with phone numbers, emails, IBANs, card numbers, Turkish ID numbers and links hidden. This content is not stored on our servers (only if you report an AI reply yourself is that reply kept, see 2.7).
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no third-party analytics or tracking tools. We don't sell your data.
  • You can delete your data from inside the app at any time: Account and data deletion.

1. Data controller

Unwrap is developed and provided by the individual developer İbrahim Aydın, who is the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: aydinibrahim25@gmail.com.

2. What data we process, and where

2.1 Data that stays on your phone

The following is stored in the app's own folder on your phone. It is not sent to us and we can't access it:

  • The chat export files you pick or share (.txt, .json, .zip) and the messages, participant names and statistics extracted from them. Files are read and parsed on the device.
  • The AI reports created for you and your conversations with Mila.
  • Your profile: name, avatar (including a photo you pick from your gallery), form of address, frame and share-card choices, details you typed in yourself, badges and app preferences.

Your profile photo stays on your phone and is never uploaded. Share cards are shared only by you, through your phone's share menu.

2.2 Data sent for AI analysis

This only happens when you ask for it: when you create a report for a chat (Unwrap Pro) or send a message to Mila.

  • Report: the relationship type you chose, which participant is you, participant names (up to 30), summary statistics (such as message counts, reply times, emoji and laugh counts) and the selected messages (date, time, author name and text). Half of the messages are the most recent ones; the rest are picked at even intervals from earlier in the chat. Media messages are not sent. The app language is sent too.
  • Mila: your questions and Mila's previous replies (last 40 messages). If you picked a chat, also that chat's summary statistics and selected messages, the relationship type, your name and the other person's name, and the role you chose (roleplay). General questions asked without picking a chat include no chat content.

Masking: phone numbers, email addresses, IBANs, card numbers, 11-digit ID numbers and links are replaced with labels such as [TELEFON] and [E-POSTA], first on your phone and again on our server. To be clear: names and the rest of the message text are not masked, and masking is rule-based, so it can miss information written in unusual ways (for example, addresses).

Processing: our server forwards the request to Anthropic's Claude API, the answer comes back to your phone and is stored only there. Our server does not write chat content, reports or Mila conversations to a database or to log files (the only exception is an AI reply you report yourself, see 2.7); it only keeps numbers (how many requests were made, how many tokens were used). Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use data received through its API to train models, and may keep it for a limited period stated in its policy for safety and abuse monitoring. To make follow-up questions about the same chat cheaper, Mila's context may be held in a temporary cache at Anthropic for a few minutes.

2.3 Optional account: Sign in with Apple (iPhone only)

You can sign in with Apple to bring your subscription and profile to a new phone. If you do:

  • We verify Apple's signed identity token and derive a pseudonymous user ID from Apple's user identifier with a one-way hash. We don't request or store your email address; the name Apple provides at first sign-in is written only to the profile on your phone.
  • The server (Upstash Redis) stores this profile: your name (up to 40 characters), your avatar (an emoji or a preset character ID plus a color, never a photo) and corrections you typed into your profile (up to 30 short entries).
  • Session record: only the SHA-256 hash of your sign-in token and your user ID; deleted automatically after 365 days.
  • Chats, reports and Mila conversations are not synced with the account; they stay on your phone.

2.4 Subscriptions and payment

Payments are taken by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card details. Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat: RevenueCat creates an anonymous user ID in the app (or uses your pseudonymous account ID if you signed in with Apple) and matches the store's purchase records to it. On each AI request our server asks RevenueCat whether your subscription is active and caches the answer for up to 5 minutes.

2.5 Security and abuse prevention

  • App verification: we check that requests come from an unmodified copy of Unwrap downloaded from the store, using Apple App Attest on iPhone and Google Play Integrity on Android. For this we store, linked to your user ID, the device's App Attest key ID, public key and signature counter on iPhone, and on Android a random installation ID generated by the app (not a hardware ID). A subscription can be used on up to 4 devices.
  • Fair-use counters: per-minute, daily and monthly request counters linked to your user ID. For Sign in with Apple, your IP address is used for the per-minute limit; that counter is deleted within 2 minutes.
  • Aggregate statistics: daily totals with no user ID (number of reports and Mila replies, tokens used) and the weekly “X chats analyzed this week” counter shown in the app.

2.6 Technical logs and updates

Vercel, which hosts our server, may log request details (IP address, time, requested path, status code) and error messages for a limited period for operations and security. The app checks Expo's servers (EAS Update) for app updates; this sends the platform, the app version and a random identifier created for update delivery.

2.7 Reporting AI content

If a report or a Mila reply is inappropriate, harmful or wrong, you can report it in the app. Only the AI text you report (up to 2,000 characters), the reason you chose, an optional short note you write (up to 300 characters) and the app language are then sent to our server. The text and note are stored with phone numbers, emails and similar details masked and without your user ID, and are used to improve AI output and to review misuse. Because reports can contain short quotes from the chat, the reported text may include such quotes. The newest 2,000 reports are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or collect an advertising ID.
  • We don't use third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and we don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell your personal data or share it for marketing.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, microphone or camera. The only photo is the one you pick from your gallery as an avatar, and it stays on your phone.
  • We don't store your chats on our servers or use them to train AI models.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service (reports, Mila, profile sync, subscriptions): performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Security, blocking tampered apps and abuse, fair-use limits: our legitimate interests (KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal obligations (e.g. responding to official requests): KVKK Art. 5/2-ç; GDPR Art. 6(1)(c).

The chats you import contain other people's messages and names. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to analyze them (see the Terms of Use). We process chats only when you ask, and only for the purposes above.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to request information about it, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad, to have it corrected if incomplete or inaccurate, to have it erased or destroyed under KVKK Art. 7 and have these actions notified to recipients, to object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Send requests to aydinibrahim25@gmail.com; we reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity. You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Deleting your data

Settings → “Delete all analyses” or “Delete Mila chats”; Profile → “Delete account”. For details and requesting deletion by email, see Account and data deletion.

10. Security

All connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Your sign-in token is kept in secure storage on your phone (iPhone Keychain / Android Keystore), and the server stores only its hash. AI requests are protected by subscription and app verification checks. No method is 100% secure; if a breach occurs, we will notify as required by law.

11. Age limit and children

Because Unwrap includes relationship analysis and roleplay, it is intended only for users aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn that we have, we will delete it.
