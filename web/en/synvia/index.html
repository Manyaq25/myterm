Features

Everything you need to stop forgetting

Add by talking

Say what’s on your mind; Synvia AI turns your voice into text and adds the tasks to your list.

Add from a screenshot

Share a screenshot of a chat (from WhatsApp, for example) and it finds the promises and dates in it.

Add from a PDF

Quotes, contracts, meeting notes: it adds the tasks and deadlines inside to your list.

Nudge the other person

If someone forgot what they promised you, AI writes a polite reminder (friendly, formal or short). You send it via WhatsApp or SMS.

Repeating tasks

Rent every month, medicine every day… Set it once; when you finish one, the app creates the next.

Handle it without opening the app

Right from the reminder notification, choose “Done”, “Snooze 1 hour” or “Tomorrow”.

AI assistant

Ask things like “Who did I promise something this week?” and it answers from your list.

12 languages

The app is available in English, Turkish, German, Arabic, Japanese and 7 more languages.

Person pages

Who’s waiting on what, at a glance

Everyone gets their own page: what you promised them, what you’re waiting for from them, what’s overdue. Call, text or WhatsApp them in one tap. Just pick their number from your contacts.

FAQ

Common questions

Is Synvia AI free?

Downloading and everyday use are free. You get 6 AI uses each month. For more, go Premium or invite friends to earn extra AI uses.

Where is my data stored?

Your list and contacts are stored only on your own phone. When you use AI features, what you send is used only at that moment to find tasks and is not stored.

Does it work offline?

Adding, editing and reminders work offline. AI features such as voice, screenshots, PDFs and the assistant need an internet connection.

Is it on Android?

Yes. Synvia AI is on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone.

How do invite codes work?

Share your code from Settings → Invite a friend. When a friend installs the app and enters your code, you get 3 extra AI uses, up to 30 in total from 10 invites. They never expire and are used once your monthly uses run out.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Data → “Delete My Account and All Data” to permanently erase everything. There’s no sign-up, so this button erases your whole list and contacts from your phone. Data deletion page
