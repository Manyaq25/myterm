## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, culturally attuned, and candidly conversational tone built specifically for Gen Z and young millennial relationships. The interface balances the playful spontaneity of an editorial story card with the refined clarity of an intimate lifestyle diary. It embraces a hybrid aesthetic: modern pastel minimalism grounded by tactile, sticker-like elements, joyful micro-interactions, and story-driven presentation modes.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Celebratory & Candid:** Relationship dynamics, inside jokes, and communication habits are decoded without shame or dry metrics; the UI feels like a trusted, witty best friend dissecting your screenshots over Turkish tea.
- **Warm & Organic:** The interface rejects sterile corporate blues and clinical dashboards in favor of soft cream canvases, sunlit pastels, and expressive badge accents.
- **Story-First & Dynamic:** High-impact metric callouts, swipeable relationship "wrapped" snapshots, and bite-sized visual recaps prioritize shareability and rapid emotional resonance.

### Visual Dialect
- **Pastel Color Blocking:** Bold, expansive surfaces in soft lilac, mint, butter yellow, and coral paired harmoniously with a comforting cream substrate.
- **Playful Micro-Details:** Generous pill radii, subtle sparkling accent icons, sticker-badge overlays, and gentle hairline outlines to anchor whimsical shapes with crisp precision.
- **High-Legibility Contrast:** Deep espresso and warm obsidian typography maintain immaculate readability, ensuring playful aesthetics never compromise accessibility.

## Colors

The palette revolves around a rich, warm culinary-inspired balance: a vibrant energetic coral (`#FF6B6B`) drives action and passion, supported by an expressive lilac (`#A78BFA`), dynamic fresh mint (`#4ADE80`), and warm butter yellow (`#FDE047`) accents. The canvas sits on an organic cream foundation (`#FAF8F5`), steering completely clear of stark surgical whites.

### Functional Roles & Accents
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm milk/cream tone serving as the non-distracting foundation across standard flows.
- **Card Tier 1 (Pure White `#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated surface cards with subtle tinting.
- **Pastel Story Containers:**
  - *Lilac Bloom (`#EDE9FE`):* Intimacy, late-night chats, frequency metrics.
  - *Butter Cup (`#FEF9C3`):* Humor, double-text alerts, playful teasing.
  - *Mint Crisp (`#DCFCE7`):* Growth, balance, green flags, positive rapport.
  - *Peach Glow (`#FFEDD5`):* Fondness, nostalgia, response time metrics.
- **Text & Hierarchy:**
  - *Espresso Ink (`#231F20`):* Primary typography, high-contrast headings, active icons.
  - *Muted Roast (`#68605C`):* Secondary descriptions, timestamps, subtext.
  - *Border Hairline (`#EFEAE4`):* Subtle division lines and low-contrast surface separations.

## Typography

Typographic treatments rely on `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all weights to deliver friendly geometric curves without tipping into juvenile novelty. Tight letter-spacing on display scales gives editorial gravity to wrapped statistics (e.g., "78% Gece Kuşu"), while generous line heights in body text ensure comfortable readability during long conversational analysis summaries.

### Typographic Rules
- **Display Weights:** Use weight `800` strictly for hero stats, percentages, wrapped recap superlatives, and category intros.
- **Headings:** Headlines use weight `700` paired with negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain punchy punchlines.
- **Turkish Diacritics:** Ensure fallback typography respects Turkish unicode variants (`ç`, `ğ`, `ı`, `ö`, `ş`, `ü`, `İ`). Do not capitalize un-dotted `i` into standard ASCII `I`.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Category chips and stickers utilize uppercase or title-cased `label-md` and `label-xs` at weight `700` or `800` to establish rapid visual anchoring.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates under a mobile-first philosophy structured around a 4-column dynamic grid on mobile viewports and an 8-column layout on tablet form factors. Full bleed card carousels, responsive aspect ratios for shareable story slides (9:16 vertical cards), and safe-area docking dictate the primary experience.

### Rhythm & Alignment
- **Canvas Margins:** Mobile edges sustain a standard `margin-mobile` of `1rem` (16px), expanding to `1.25rem` (20px) on larger handheld devices.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Inner card padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) for prominent story displays, providing an uncluttered, spacious breathing field around heavy metric typography.
- **List & Metric Stacks:** Micro-gap tokens (`space-sm` / 8px) handle title-to-subtitle links; module groupings utilize `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
- **Navigation Insets:** Floating pill navigation and floating action buttons require a minimum 24px bottom buffer above the iOS Home Indicator safe area.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects intense, murky drop shadows in favor of ambient colored glows, crisp micro-borders, and tactile layered planes. Surfaces mimic tactile cardstock and lightweight stickers placed on a warm desk.

### Depth Archetypes
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Off-white base (`#FAF8F5`) with zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Content Card / List Surface):** Pure white or pastel fill with a 1px solid border in `#EFEAE4` or tonal border (e.g., `#E9D5FF` for lilac cards) and a whisper ambient shadow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Bottom Sheets):** Dynamic floating navigation pills, action sheets, and modal bottom drawers carry a soft diffused shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Stickers & Playful Badges):** Accent chips and analysis badges use a tiny offset pop shadow reminiscent of physical paper stickers: `0px 2px 0px rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces level 3 pill-shaped curvature. Extreme roundedness softens the emotional stakes of relationship analysis, transforming potentially stressful stats into a welcoming, gamified experience.

### Radius Implementations
- **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Full pill encapsulation (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for action buttons, filter tags, and status labels.
- **Story Analysis Cards:** Oversized roundedness (`rounded-3xl` / 32px to 40px) to evoke modern smartphone display aesthetics and seamless Instagram/Spotify Wrapped story framing.
- **Input Fields & Bottom Sheets:** `rounded-2xl` (24px) on form elements; top corners of modal sheets set to `rounded-t-[36px]`.
- **Micro-Borders:** A consistent 1px to 1.5px hairline outline reinforces boundaries without visually weighting down soft pastel fills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill Hero):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), Coral (`#FF6B6B`) background, pure white text, bold typography (`label-lg`). Height: 54px. Active scale down effect: `scale(0.97)` on touch.
- **Secondary / Soft Action:** Pastel-tinted surfaces (e.g., `#EDE9FE` Lilac) with high-contrast espresso text (`#231F20`). No harsh shadows.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with a 1.5px hairline border (`#231F20` or `#EFEAE4`) and espresso text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Sticker Chips:** Rounded pill shapes used for tags like "Flirty", "Gece Kuşu", "Ghost Riski", or "Bağlanma Stili". Tinted pastel base, 1px tone-on-tone border, accompanied by playful emoji or vector sparkle glyphs.
- **Counter Badges:** Minimalistic circular/capsule pills displaying raw analysis counts with bold high-contrast numbers.

### Story & Wrapped Cards
- **9:16 Shareable Cards:** Immersive full-screen or carousel cards featuring high-impact pastel gradients (e.g., Peach-to-Lilac mesh). 
- **Typography Layout:** Large metric numbers (`display-xl-mobile`) locked in center stage with an editorial commentary snippet positioned in a pill sticker above or below.
- **Header Slot:** Mini user avatars or duo-avatar bubble links with chat metadata.

### Input Fields & Upload Drops
- **Chat Upload Container:** Dashed micro-border (`#D1C7BD`), warm cream background, accompanied by an animated sticker icon prompting WhatsApp / Telegram / Instagram export file drops.
- **Text Inputs:** Soft white base, 52px height, generous internal padding (`space-md`), pill or `24px` corner radius, zero focus glow—focus indicated via a subtle 1.5px espresso border shift.

### Lists & Relationship Summaries
- **Chat Breakdown Rows:** White pill-shaped segmented list items separated by `space-sm` gaps rather than dividing lines.
- **Progress Trackers:** Fat, ultra-rounded horizontal progress meters (12px height, full pill radius) featuring pastel fills (Mint for response speed, Lilac for word volume, Coral for affection score).

### Bottom Navigation & App Bar
- **Floating Island Bar:** Floating pill navigation docked 20px above the bottom screen safe area. Translucent warm off-white glass (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)`) with backdrop blur (`blur(16px)`), framed with a hairline border (`rgba(35, 31, 32, 0.06)`).
- **Icons:** Dual-tone or filled playful line icons that scale up slightly when selected with a tiny butter yellow or coral dot indicator underneath.
